How we recover an account

NRAM's industry experience has shaped a distinctive credit management approach — tailored to each business's operations and cost considerations, to minimise expense for our clients.

  1. 01

    Initial measure

    Accounts received from clients are assessed on collectability and risk before being distributed to a collector.

  2. 02

    Information gathering & preparation

    Our trained recovery officers determine whether the account information is sufficient to begin collection. If not, a visit and/or skip tracing is conducted.

  3. 03

    Collection calls & negotiation

    Calls are made by professional recovery officers well-versed in negotiation, offering repayment options. Every call is documented and reviewed for consistency and quality.

  4. 04

    Skip tracing

    We continue tracing debtors even where there's been no payment for years, or a debtor has left their last known address — using our resources and, where needed, visitation.

  5. 05

    Field visit

    Where a debtor cannot be reached by phone, our officers arrange a field visit to their office, business or home to help locate and engage them.

  6. 06

    Legal assistance

    NRAM has an in-house lawyer to assist clients on all legal matters regarding their debts, where escalation becomes necessary.
